How do I start recording?

Prepare your script, tap Continue and grant camera and microphone access. Read the script on the camera screen, or use the outer view when supported. Tap Record. The take starts after the countdown.

Where are my videos?

Recordings stay in the app. Open an internal recording and choose Save to Photos. Only add access is requested. Export important videos before deleting the app.

Why is no text visible outside?

The feature needs the open pose, the app full-screen inside, and an active camera. Folding, backgrounding or interruptions can remove the outer view. Returning never starts a recording automatically. Recording and on-screen script playback also work without the outer view.

Which files can I import?

TXT, RTF, DOCX and PDF via the native document picker. Scanned PDF pages can be recognized locally. Check the preview before replacing or appending text. Handwriting, complex layouts and protected files may have limitations.
